About My Research

My research sits at the intersection of agentic AI and human experience. Specifically, I study how autonomous systems (computer-using agents, agentic pipelines, multi-agent orchestration) reshape how people build and interact with software, and what that means for usability, developer workflows, and the security and authorization challenges that emerge when agents act on behalf of users.
